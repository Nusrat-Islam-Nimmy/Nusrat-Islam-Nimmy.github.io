Nusrat Islam

I am an M.Sc. student in Statistics and Data Science at Islamic University, Bangladesh, and a Research Assistant at the Statistical Intelligence Research Lab. My research interests lie at the intersection of statistical learning, machine learning, deep learning, medical image analysis, computer vision, and AI for healthcare. My research has resulted in peer-reviewed publications in PLOS ONE and IEEE conferences, and I am actively involved in ongoing work on medical image segmentation, deep learning architecture design, and novel activation functions.

My current methodological interest focuses on developing novel activation functions for deep learning and investigating their behavior across convolutional and transformer-based architectures.
