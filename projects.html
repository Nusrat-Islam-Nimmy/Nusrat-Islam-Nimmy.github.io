Medical Image Segmentation
2026

PVTv2-DVRNet

PVTv2-DVRNet: A Dynamic Vessel Refinement Network for Accurate Retinal Vessel Segmentation in Fundus Images.

This project investigates a PVTv2-based architecture for retinal vessel segmentation, with emphasis on fine vessels, vessel continuity, multi-scale contextual information, and edge-aware feature refinement.

Status: Manuscript in preparation · Submitted for NST Fellowship 2026–27 fiscal year.

STanh: A Novel Self-Gated Activation Function for Robust and Efficient Deep Learning Across Convolutional and Transformer Architectures.

The work studies how activation-function design can influence optimization, nonlinear representation learning, and model behavior across convolutional and transformer architectures.
